Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Email ebrahimkalas@gmail.com. It helps to include your iPhone model, the game version (Settings → Updates) and what happened.

Common questions

How do I move my progress to a new phone?
Sign in with the same Apple ID and iCloud: your save follows you. You can also use Settings → Save & Backup → Copy save code, then paste it on the new device.

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it back?
Open Settings → Ads → Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices (Europe and UK), or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
