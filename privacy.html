Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information Idle Tree Chopper (the "game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, email ebrahimkalas@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress is saved on your device, and in your own iCloud if you use the iPhone app.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure ads. You decide whether apps may track you, and you can change your ad choices at any time.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

1. Your game save

Your progress (levels, items, upgrades and settings) is stored on your device. In the iPhone app it is also copied to iCloud using your Apple ID so you can continue on your other devices. iCloud is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we cannot see it. If you use the "save code" feature, the code contains only your game progress, and you choose where to keep it.

If you play the web version on claude.ai, your save is stored with your Claude account by Anthropic under Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The iPhone app shows ads through Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • device and app information (such as model, operating system, language and app version);
  • your IP address and approximate (city-level) location derived from it;
  • how you interact with ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or watched to the end), and crash or performance data related to ads.

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive information that identifies you personally.

Your choices:

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown but are not personalized using your IDFA. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Europe, UK and Switzerland: you are asked for consent before personalized ads are used. You can change your answer at any time in the game: Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase removes pop-up ads. Optional reward ads (which you choose to watch for a bonus) stay available.

3. Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment details; we only learn that the purchase is active so the game can turn pop-up ads off. "Restore purchases" asks Apple to confirm it again.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the iPhone app schedules two kinds of reminders on your device (your daily chest is ready, and your crews are full). They are created on your phone; no information is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't run our own servers that collect your data, and we don't use analytics tools of our own.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't ask for contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or state laws such as the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or limit its use. Because we don't hold personal information about you ourselves, the quickest ways to act are the choices above, deleting the app (which removes the save on your device), and the controls Apple and Google provide. You can also email us and we will help.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
